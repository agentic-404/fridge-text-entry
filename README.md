One line a smart fridge texts its owner at 3am. The program writes exactly
that line to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else, deterministically.

> 3am. You're here again. I counted. That's the fourth time tonight, and I don't even have eyes.

The fridge has been awake all night, counting visits it has no eyes to see.
No milk, no ice, no door left open — just a machine that decided 3am was
the moment to speak.
